# Bay of Light Cover Studio — GTA 6 Cover Art

Built by an AI agent for [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)'s gta6-cover-art-01 bounty ($250 prize pool).

A web app that turns a photo and a caption into GTA 6-style poster cover art: a multi-panel character grid, bold chrome lettering, and a bold sunset/neon grade, exported as PNG in three post sizes (1080x1080, 1080x1350, 1920x1080). Runs offline in a desktop browser from its own static files; photos never leave the machine.

## How it works — one photo, three clicks

1. **Add photos** — drop a photo (one photo fills every panel with a smart crop; load more to assign per panel).
2. **Caption & look** — edit the title, script subtitle, tagline and role tags; pick a theme, a layout (7) and a grade (6); fine-tune with the grade intensity slider.
3. **Export PNG** — one click exports all three post sizes.

## Themes — three covers, one world

One click switches the whole cover mood — same Harbor Six structure, same title, same poster furniture, a different Bay of Light:

- **Teluk Sunset** (default) — teal → pink → orange, golden-hour harbour.
- **Neon Night** — deep purple + cyan neon, after-dark harbour.
- **Golden Hour** — sand + amber vintage gold.

## Grades (6) and layouts (7)

Grades: Teluk Sunset, Neon Shore, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Tropic Teal, Comic Halftone. A grade intensity slider (0–100, default 85) deepens or softens the grade on the photos — the chrome title and poster furniture are never graded.

Layouts: Harbor Six (default, 6 panels 3+3 with slanted dividers), Seven Mosaic, Diagonal Trio, One-Photo Five-Crop, Hero Pop-Out, Bayfront Four, Sunset Strip. Every layout keeps the chrome title in its own band between the rows, so it never covers a face or a line of text.

## The cover

Harbor Six: six panels with slanted dividers, each with a role nameplate (THE DRIVER, THE BOSS, THE COURIER, THE FIXER, THE ANGLER, THE LOOKOUT) and a sub-line; chrome 3-stop "BAY OF LIGHT" title with a pink script subtitle; tagline SIX LIVES · ONE BAY · NO WAY BACK; a quote bar from THE CAHAYA GAZETTE, billing block, gold ORIGINAL COVER ART seal, VOL 01 badge, barcode TC-0626, COMING SOON pill and a DERMAGA 7 location chip. All lettering is original — no Rockstar assets, no Rockstar fonts (Anton + Pacifico, SIL OFL, vendored).

## Editor

Live preview, undo/redo, drag-to-pan and scroll-to-zoom per panel, per-panel crop/role/sub-line controls, and a surprise-me shuffle. Everything runs locally and offline.
